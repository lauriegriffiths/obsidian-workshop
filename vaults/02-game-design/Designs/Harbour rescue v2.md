# Harbour rescue v2

Revision of [[Harbour rescue v1]], responding to [[Playtest 01]].

Keep the 2–4 player, 15-minute cooperative rescue premise.

## Changed rules

Before the clock starts, allow one open planning round. During each rescue round, choose one token: danger, clear, wait, or help. Then move one boat. Rescue three crews within six rounds.

## Why change it?

A shared vocabulary may reduce negotiation and help quieter players contribute. Private map information still creates uncertainty.

## Next test

Does every player contribute by round two? Record moments of useful cooperation and moments of confusion.

This is a dated design decision, not an automatic file versioning system.
