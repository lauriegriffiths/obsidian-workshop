# Harbour rescue v1

A cooperative tabletop game for 2–4 players, about 15 minutes.

## Premise

Guide rescue boats through a foggy harbour. Each player knows part of the route.

## First rules

Each round, players may each give one spoken word, then move one boat. Rescue three stranded crews before the six-round clock ends.

## Why this idea?

[[Limited communication]] connects the references. I want uncertainty to create cooperation.

## Question to test

Do players feel intriguingly constrained, or simply confused?
